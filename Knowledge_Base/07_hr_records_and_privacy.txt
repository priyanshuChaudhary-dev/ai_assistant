SAMPLE COMPANY POLICY: HR RECORDS AND PRIVACY

Purpose
This fictional guide explains basic handling expectations for Sample Company personnel records. Applicable privacy, employment, and records-retention requirements vary by location.

Access and use
- Access employee and candidate information only when needed for an authorized work responsibility.
- Use approved HR systems and secure channels to collect, view, or share HR records.
- Do not store sensitive HR documents in personal email, consumer file-sharing accounts, or unapproved devices.
- Verify the recipient before sending records and share only the minimum information required.

Requests and incidents
Employees should direct requests to access or correct their HR information to People Operations. Suspected misdirected documents, unauthorized access, or loss of HR records should be reported promptly to People Operations and the designated privacy or security contact.

Retention and disposal
People Operations manages retention and secure disposal under the approved records schedule and applicable law. Employees should not independently delete, retain, or copy HR records outside authorized systems.

This sample is not a complete privacy notice and does not define specific retention periods.
