SAMPLE COMPANY POLICY: ATTENDANCE AND FLEXIBLE WORK

Purpose
This fictional policy describes basic expectations for attendance and flexible work at Sample Company. Team arrangements may differ according to role and location.

Employees should agree on their normal schedule and work location with their manager, keep calendars reasonably current, and attend required meetings or notify the organizer if they cannot attend. If delayed or unexpectedly unavailable, employees should contact their manager as soon as practical.

Flexible or remote work
- Confirm recurring remote or flexible arrangements with the manager.
- Maintain a safe, private work environment and protect company information from being viewed or heard by others.
- Use approved devices and collaboration tools for work.
- Coordinate availability and handoffs with teammates, taking time zones into account.

Managers should communicate expectations consistently and consider requests fairly. Any formal accommodation or leave request should be referred to People Operations.

This sample does not establish a guaranteed remote-work entitlement or replace local workplace rules.
