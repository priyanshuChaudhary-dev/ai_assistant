SAMPLE COMPANY POLICY: NEW EMPLOYEE ONBOARDING

Purpose
This fictional onboarding guide helps new employees understand a typical first-week process at Sample Company.

Before the start date
- The manager confirms the employee's role, start date, work location, and first-week schedule.
- People Operations provides required forms and explains how to submit them securely.
- IT prepares approved equipment and access based on the employee's role.

First week
1. The manager welcomes the employee and introduces key teammates.
2. People Operations explains benefits enrollment, payroll setup, workplace policies, and where to ask HR questions.
3. IT helps the employee set up accounts and reviews basic security practices.
4. The manager agrees on initial goals, training, and regular check-ins.
5. The employee reviews the employee handbook and acknowledges receipt of required policies.

Managers should make reasonable efforts to provide accessible training and a clear point of contact. Employees should report missing access, equipment issues, or questions to their manager or the relevant support team.

This sample does not specify legal documentation requirements; those vary by location and must be handled through approved company procedures.
