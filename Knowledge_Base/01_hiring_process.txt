SAMPLE COMPANY POLICY: HIRING PROCESS

Purpose
This guide describes a consistent, fair hiring process for Sample Company. It is a fictional example for knowledge-base and testing use; it is not legal advice or an official policy.

Process
1. The hiring manager documents the role's responsibilities, required skills, and compensation range before a job is posted.
2. Recruiting shares the approved description through appropriate channels and provides candidates with an accessible way to request interview accommodations.
3. Applications are reviewed against the published role requirements. Candidate assessments should focus on job-related skills.
4. A typical interview process includes an initial recruiter conversation, a structured skills interview, and a conversation with the hiring manager or team.
5. Interviewers use consistent questions and record factual, role-related feedback promptly. Personal characteristics unrelated to the role must not influence decisions.
6. The hiring manager reviews feedback with Recruiting before a decision is made. Recruiting communicates outcomes to candidates respectfully.
7. Any offer must be approved by the appropriate manager and People Operations before it is sent in writing.

Confidentiality
Candidate resumes, interview notes, and contact details are confidential and should only be accessed by people involved in the hiring process. Retain or delete records according to the organization's approved records schedule and applicable law.

Questions about this sample process should be directed to People Operations.
