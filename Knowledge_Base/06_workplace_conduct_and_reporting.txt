SAMPLE COMPANY POLICY: WORKPLACE CONDUCT AND REPORTING

Purpose
Sample Company expects a professional, inclusive, and respectful workplace. This fictional sample is provided for demonstration and does not replace the organization's official policies or legal obligations.

Employees should communicate respectfully, treat colleagues fairly, protect confidential information, and avoid conduct that intimidates, discriminates against, or harasses others.

Raising a concern
- Employees may speak with their manager, People Operations, or another designated reporting contact.
- If the concern involves the employee's manager, use another available reporting channel.
- Reports should be made in good faith and include relevant facts, dates, and witnesses where known.
- The company should review concerns promptly and as impartially as practical, sharing information only with those who need it to respond.

Retaliation against a person for raising a concern in good faith or participating in a review is not acceptable. Anyone who believes retaliation has occurred should report it through an available channel.

For immediate safety concerns, contact local emergency services or the designated workplace safety contact.
