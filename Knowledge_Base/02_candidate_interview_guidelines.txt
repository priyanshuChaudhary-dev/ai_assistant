SAMPLE COMPANY POLICY: CANDIDATE INTERVIEW GUIDELINES

Purpose
These guidelines support respectful, structured interviews at Sample Company. This fictional sample is for demonstration only and does not replace local employment requirements.

Before the interview
- Review the role description and agree on the skills the interview will assess.
- Prepare consistent, job-related questions and tell the candidate what to expect.
- Confirm the meeting details and ask whether an accommodation would help the candidate participate.

During the interview
- Introduce participants and explain the format and expected duration.
- Give candidates time to ask questions and describe their experience.
- Avoid questions about protected or personal topics that are not relevant to the work.
- Do not request confidential information about a candidate's current or former employer.

After the interview
- Submit independent, evidence-based feedback using the agreed criteria.
- Keep notes professional, factual, and limited to information relevant to the role.
- Store interview materials only in approved recruiting systems; do not share them through personal accounts or informal channels.

Hiring decisions should be based on role requirements and documented evidence, not on a single interviewer's unsupported impression.
