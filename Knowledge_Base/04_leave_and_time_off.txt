SAMPLE COMPANY POLICY: LEAVE AND TIME OFF

Purpose
This fictional sample summarizes a general time-off request process for Sample Company. Actual leave entitlements and procedures depend on the employee's location, applicable law, and the official plan documents.

Requesting planned time off
- Submit a request through the approved HR or scheduling system as early as practical.
- Include the dates requested and any handoff information needed for active work.
- The manager reviews coverage and responds through the system. Employees should wait for confirmation before treating planned time off as approved.

Unexpected absence
If an employee cannot work because of illness or an emergency, they should notify their manager as soon as reasonably possible and follow the local call-in procedure. Medical details should be shared only through the appropriate confidential HR channel when needed.

Protected and statutory leave
Employees should contact People Operations for information about legally protected, family, medical, or other statutory leave. Such requests should be handled confidentially and in accordance with applicable requirements. Managers must refer questions about eligibility or documentation to People Operations rather than making assumptions.

This sample is not a promise of a specific number of leave days and does not override applicable law or official company plan documents.
